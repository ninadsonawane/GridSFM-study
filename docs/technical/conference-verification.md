# Conference requirements verified 21 October 2026

The current [2027 call](https://pes-gm.org/2027-san-francisco/2027-call-for-papers/) and linked [CFP flyer, p.2](https://pes-gm.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/6.8.26-Flyer_2027-IEEE-PES-GM-CFP.pdf) state 25–29 July 2027 in San Francisco and a full-paper deadline of **10 November 2026**, at most five pages. No extension is established. The old Montreal-hosted page has a stale 2025 footer/link; use the new 2027 page and its PESGM2027 submission link.

[PES Author's Kit Part 3, Technical Work Preparation](https://ieee-pes.org/publications/authors-kit/preparation-and-submission-of-conference-technical-papers/) explicitly includes references, attributions, figures and tables in the five pages. Author names/affiliations must appear; embed PDF fonts and do not password-protect the submission. Use the official [PES conference templates](https://ieee-pes.org/publications/authors-kit/templates-and-sample-of-pes-technical-papers/), not the Transactions template. The [sample, p.1](https://ieee-pes.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/pg4-sample-conference-paper.pdf) specifies US Letter and preserving template margins, columns, spacing and fonts; final content is two columns. [Part 4b](https://ieee-pes.org/publications/authors-kit/preparation-of-a-formatted-conference-paper/) limits the abstract to 150 words and keywords to five. Do not shrink the template to fit.

The page labels this “EXTENDED TO” but gives the same date as the current call; no later extension is assumed. No account was accessed and no submission created.

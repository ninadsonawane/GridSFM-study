Frozen GridSFM v1.1: inference, failure diagnosis and solver initialization
Completed 21 October 2026. Scope: frozen pretrained v1.1 only; no LoRA, fine-tuning, modified impedances or relaxed limits. Existing numerical results were preserved. Findings below cover two nominal target-network operating points and a five-point case300 initialization pilot, not a generalization or speedup benchmark.
Main finding: raw GridSFM predictions are physically infeasible on case300, the connected 600-bus system, and the shipped 500-bus control. No unresolved conversion, unit, ordering or evaluator error was found. Model-side physics differences are confirmed, but do not fully establish the cause of poor frozen transfer. GridSFM-based initialization provided no benefit over the default solver initialization in this pilot.
1. Inputs, model and numerical reference
Original network: official MATPOWER 8.1 case300, 300 buses, 69 generators and 411 branches, 100 MVA system base. MATPOWER source commit: 1a828c7af590714499284e36ee9c81273388c594. Source SHA-256: 69a90280e999ef533d94656e0fbc08311f1347c962dd2753ff2005ff5e3f9ac5. All original RATE_A values are zero, meaning unlimited; no internal line rating was inferred.
Constructed network: two unchanged case300 copies, 600 buses, 138 generators and 824 separate physical branches. Two parallel circuits connect original bus 231 in area A to original bus 231 in area B (both 345 kV). Each circuit uses r=0.002 pu, x=0.019 pu, total charging b=1.127 pu and a provisional synthetic 600 MVA rating. The 822 copied interior branches remain unlimited. One reference bus remains and the former second reference retains PV voltage-control status. Both ties are available and original loads are unchanged.
Frozen model: local Seiole GridSFM fork commit 1e5e3a1640b27768e3eaf32680184f26b407caab; pretrained Microsoft GridSFM Open v1.1, 15,148,227 parameters. Checkpoint SHA-256: f8a4396122e603e8303afdebe3b093819c0f64dac0878394aed0bd63205fd831. Study starting commit: c74c2bd1685108f7c991274ef7a09d4c52e8f2fb. Exact runner and model-source hashes accompany the artifacts; installed model code was unchanged.
Reference solver: PowerModels 0.21.5 polar AC-OPF with Ipopt.jl 1.14.0 under Julia 1.11.9. MATPOWER parsing disables default network corrections and performs only per-unit conversion; disabled ±360° angle constraints are omitted. No load shedding, inferred rating, voltage/Q-limit widening or other feasibility relaxation is used. References are independently checked local solutions, not global optimality certificates; these were not MATPOWER/MIPS executions.

4. Failure diagnosis and pipeline evidence
Previously completed conversion, branch identity, cycle rank and label-independence checks were reused. Raw predictions repeat bitwise and are unchanged when accepted solution labels are added. Bus IDs are not assumed to be row indices; case300x2 uses explicit original-ID and area mappings. Generator order is preserved. Power is converted with the 100 MVA system base (not individual generator MBASE); radians become degrees only at the physical evaluator boundary; cost coefficients are reversed consistently. Source and reconstructed electrical parameters/objectives/solved flows agree. Evidence is retained in checks.json, analyze.py, verify_inference.py and reference-export-reload-check.json.
Small-impedance mismatch: loss.py floors r²+x² at 10⁻⁶. It affects original branches 37–9001, 231–237 and 235–238, retaining only 21.52%, 37% and 40% of their true series admittance; the duplicated system has six affected circuits. On exactly the same predicted state, case300 maximum P/Q residuals become 1,946.06 MW / 4,433.47 MVAr instead of exact 3,754.08 / 8,937.97. Case300x2 becomes 645.46 / 2,955.75 instead of 1,141.64 / 9,376.45. Even exact feasible references falsely acquire about 244 MW maximum residual under this loss formulation. Exact original-parameter metrics remain authoritative.
The forward flow calculation instead uses r²+x²+10⁻¹². Isolating this change on the same states changes maximum bus residuals by at most 0.00788 MW / 0.02494 MVAr for case300 and 0.00339 MW / 0.04156 MVAr for case300x2. Actual saved FP32 branch-flow differences from exact physics are at most 0.03194 / 0.04025 MVA. These discrepancies cannot account for the thousands of MW/MVAr of imbalance. Actual torch loss/forward functions agree with diagnostic float64 complex-admittance calculations within 1.2×10⁻¹⁰ in power units.
Graph context also floors r²+x² at 10⁻⁶, clipping the target networks’ maximum admittance feature from 2,155.65 to 1,000 pu. This is a clipped input statistic, not an exact flow computation. The separate |x|<10⁻¹⁰ DC/positional-feature safeguards do not activate here. No valid effective tap triggers the tap fallback.
Unlimited-rating mismatch: stress_features.py treats zero RATE_A as 10⁻⁹ pu. The 411/822 unlimited branches produce false maximum loading ratios of approximately 8.48×10¹⁰ / 8.94×10¹⁰. These features enter the feasibility head after voltage/generation outputs have been computed; they undermine interpretation of its 0.0 score but cannot themselves explain the dispatch errors. No claim is made that this alone causes the entire classifier output.
Diagnosis: confirmed model-side loss/feature/limit-semantics differences, together with poor frozen transfer and strong sensitivity near small impedances. The full causal origin of dispatch error remains uncertain; errors remain severe even under the clamped loss calculation. These findings are not evidence that the case is infeasible, that conversion failed, or that simply reaching 600 buses makes a topology in distribution.

At 0.90–1.05, all repeats reach independently feasible local solutions with matching objectives (difference below 7×10⁻⁸ cost units/hour). Across those runs the worst final P/Q residuals are below 3×10⁻¹⁰ MW / 4.1×10⁻⁹ MVAr. GridSFM initialization takes more iterations and more end-to-end time at every successful load point. There is no observed initialization benefit.
At 1.10, both methods return LOCALLY_INFEASIBLE in all repeats. Final maximum reactive imbalances are 33.13364 MVAr (default) and 33.24586 MVAr (GridSFM). Diagnostic objectives 846,779.47966 / 846,809.27624 are retained but are not feasible operating costs or accepted reference labels. A failed local solve is not proof of network infeasibility. No fallback was used.
Identical settings: Ipopt tol=10⁻⁹, constr_viol_tol=10⁻¹⁰, acceptable_tol=10⁻⁹, max_iter=1000, max_cpu_time=180 s, bound_relax_factor=0. No solved labels enter inference. At nominal load, newly timed predictions exactly match the saved predictions. Raw predictions are evaluated separately; no accepted cost-error baseline exists at 1.10.
GridSFM initialization: clip only Vm/Pg/Qg to original box limits, anchor angles to the original reference, and initialize branch primal powers from exact π-model flows at those voltages. No PF projection or balance repair is performed. At nominal load, explicit clipping changes one Vm by 2.38×10⁻⁹ pu and five Qg values by at most 4.77×10⁻⁶ MVAr. Default starts are Vm=1, angles=0, Pg/Qg/branch flows=0 before fixed-variable/bound handling.
Start consumption was verified from both actual JuMP starts and Ipopt’s internal unscaled iteration-zero primal via GetIpoptCurrentIterate. Nominal internal shifts from supplied GridSFM starts are at most 0.0012 pu Vm, 0 radians, 0.01 pu Pg and 0.012 pu Qg, reflecting normal interior/fixed-variable handling. Recomputed initial costs match callback objectives: default 1,644.236368319 and GridSFM 324,330.435171679. Thus the supplied initialization was actually consumed; the observed lack of benefit is not explained by ignored starts.
End-to-end timing includes input read; conversion, serialization, model preprocessing/inference when applicable; clipping/branch-start calculation; server transport; MATPOWER parse/per-unit conversion; optimization-model construction; solve; and raw-result serialization. Independent post-solve evaluation/report generation is outside the timer. Warm topology caches are reused; disk-cache restrictions caused an in-memory fallback. Setup, checkpoint load and first-call compilation costs are separate. This is a local pilot, not a broad acceleration claim.

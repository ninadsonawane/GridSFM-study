## Candidate interpretations to reject unless stronger evidence emerges

- "Neural outputs can violate physics despite small state errors": established.
- "PF completion can leave Q, voltage, slack-P or flow violations": established, including the closest sensitivity-informed OPF paper and GridSFM itself.
- "Independent P/Q perturbations reveal response problems": insufficient alone; the recent AC-PF geometry paper includes independent perturbations and response diagnostics.
- "Newton convergence is a learned-model benefit": false attribution; completion enforces equations numerically.
- "A new network/plot combination is a contribution": unsupported.

The bounded question left for our data is whether specific raw-Q and distributional findings survive the actual completed stage, after excluding representation and slack-mapping artifacts, and which practical interpretation of this released workflow should change.
